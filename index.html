THE CLINICAL PROBLEM

Diameter measures size. Not strength.

Current assessment relies heavily on generalized anatomical risk markers, yet patients with similar aortic dimensions can have very different underlying tissue vulnerability.

HOW IT WORKS

From imaging to biomechanical insight.

Aortico combines cardiovascular imaging, biomechanics, and artificial intelligence to characterize the mechanical vulnerability of the aortic wall.
